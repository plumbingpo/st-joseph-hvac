Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Saint Louis

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
